Types of Triangles

Definition: A triangle is a fundamental polygon with three edges and three vertices. It is one of the basic shapes in geometry.

Triangles are classified in two main ways: by the lengths of their sides (Equilateral, Isosceles, Scalene) and by their interior angles (Acute, Right, Obtuse).
