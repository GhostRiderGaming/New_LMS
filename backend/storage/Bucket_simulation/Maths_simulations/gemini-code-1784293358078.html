Arc on Circles

An arc is a connected portion of the circumference of a circle. Its size and length depend entirely on the circle's radius and the central angle it subtends.

Understanding the Arc

Imagine slicing a pie. The curved crust on the edge of your slice is the Arc. The straight edges are the Radii, and the angle between them at the center is the Central Angle.

  • Minor Arc: Less than a semicircle.
  • Major Arc: Greater than a semicircle.

Scientific Fact

Over 2,200 years ago, the Greek polymath Eratosthenes calculated the circumference of the Earth with remarkable accuracy. He did this by measuring the shadow angle of the sun in two different cities, effectively calculating the arc length of the Earth's surface between them!
