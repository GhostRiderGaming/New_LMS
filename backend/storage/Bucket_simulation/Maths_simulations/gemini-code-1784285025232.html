Concept Definition

A circle is geometrically defined as all points in a 2D plane at a fixed distance (Radius) from a center point. The interaction between straight lines and circles forms the foundation of trigonometry and calculus.

Did you know? The word "tangent" comes from the Latin word tangere, meaning "to touch", perfectly describing how the line elegantly kisses the circle at a single infinite point!
