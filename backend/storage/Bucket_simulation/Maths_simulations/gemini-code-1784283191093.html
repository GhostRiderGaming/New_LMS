Midpoint Theorem

Interactive Geometric Laboratory. Drag the vertices to explore the mathematical relationships in real-time.

Definition

The line segment in a triangle connecting the midpoints of two sides is strictly parallel to the third side, and its length is exactly half of the third side.

💡 Fact: This theorem is a special case of the intercept theorem (Thales's theorem) applied to a ratio of 1:1.
