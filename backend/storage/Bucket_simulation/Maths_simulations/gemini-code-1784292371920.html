Congruent Figures Properties

Interactive Geometric Laboratory

⚡ Definition

Two geometric figures are congruent (≅) if they have the exact same shape and size. For polygons, this means all corresponding sides and angles are equal.

Moving, rotating, or reflecting a figure does not change its congruence.

∑ Mathematical Notation

If Triangle ABC is congruent to Triangle DEF, we write:

ΔABC ≅ ΔDEF
Implies corresponding parts are equal (CPCTC):
AB = DE, BC = EF, AC = DF
∠A = ∠D, ∠B = ∠E, ∠C = ∠F

★ Geometric Fact

You don't need to measure all 6 parts (3 sides, 3 angles) to prove triangles are congruent. Mathematics provides shortcuts based on rigid structural properties.

Rules: SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, and RHS.
