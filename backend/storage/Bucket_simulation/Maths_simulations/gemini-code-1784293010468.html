Concept Overview

Trigonometry links the geometry of right-angled triangles to the mathematics of continuous, periodic phenomena. By tracking a point rotating around a unit circle, we can unfold its vertical (Sine) and horizontal (Cosine) positions into smooth, infinite waves.

The equations governing waves and oscillations take the form:
y(t) = A · sin(ωt + φ)

Did you know? The word "sine" comes from a historical mistranslation. The original Sanskrit word jiva (bowstring) was translated to Arabic as jiba, misread as jaib (fold/bay), and translated to Latin as sinus!
