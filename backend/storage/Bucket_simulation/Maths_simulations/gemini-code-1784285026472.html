Polynomial Lab

Graphical representations of cubic, quadratic, and linear functions.

Theory

A polynomial graph forms a continuous, smooth curve. The degree determines its shape: straight line (deg 1), parabola (deg 2), or an S-curve (deg 3).

f(x) = ax³ + bx² + cx + d f'(x) = 3ax² + 2bx + c (Derivative/Slope)
💡 Fact: A polynomial of degree n has at most n real roots and n-1 turning points (extrema).
