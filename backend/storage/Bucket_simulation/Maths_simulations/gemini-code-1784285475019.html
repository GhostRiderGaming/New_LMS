Geometrical Meaning of Zeroes

Interactive Laboratory for Polynomial Analysis

Definition

The zeroes (or roots) of a polynomial P(x) are the specific values of x for which the polynomial equals zero.


Geometrically, the zeroes are the exact x-coordinates where the graph of y = P(x) intersects the X-axis.

Important Formulas

Quadratic: y = ax2 + bx + c
Roots: x =
−b ± √b2 − 4ac 2a
Cubic: y = ax3 + bx2 + cx + d

Fun Fact

The Fundamental Theorem of Algebra states that a polynomial of degree n always has exactly n complex roots.


However, only the Real Roots appear on the X-axis! If a quadratic graph floats above or below the axis, its roots exist, but they are imaginary numbers.
