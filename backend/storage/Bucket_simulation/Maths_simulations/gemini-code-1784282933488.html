Heron's Formula

A method in geometry to calculate the area of any triangle when the lengths of all three sides are known.

Unlike traditional area formulas (like A = 12 b×h), Heron's formula requires no angles and no altitudes. You only need the three boundary lengths: a, b, and c.

📐 Mathematical Equations

s = a + b + c 2
Area = √ s (s − a) (s − b) (s − c)

*Where s is the semi-perimeter (half of the triangle's total perimeter).

💡 Laboratory Fact

Hero of Alexandria, a Greek mathematician, engineer, and inventor in 60 AD, is widely credited with this formula. He published it in his book Metrica. However, historical evidence suggests the great mathematician Archimedes may have known this formula over two centuries earlier!
