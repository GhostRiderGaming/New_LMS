Section Formula Laboratory

Definition: The section formula in coordinate geometry is used to find the exact coordinates of a point that divides a line segment joining two given points into a specific ratio.

Concept: Imagine a straight laser beam connecting Point A and Point B. If you want to place a sensor Point P such that the distance from A to P and P to B is strictly in the ratio m : n, this formula gives you the exact mathematical coordinates.

Internal Division:
P(x, y) = (
mx2X-coordinate of Point B + nx1X-coordinate of Point A m + n
,
my2Y-coordinate of Point B + ny1Y-coordinate of Point A m + n
)
