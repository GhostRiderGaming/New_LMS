Interactive mathematical modeling of three-dimensional geometric primitives in Cartesian space.

Definitions & Mechanics

A Polyhedron is a solid in 3D bounded by flat polygonal faces, straight edges, and sharp vertices. The simulation computes 3D coordinates $(x,y,z)$ and uses Euler angle rotation matrices alongside perspective projection to render the object onto a 2D plane.

Fact: There are only exactly five regular convex polyhedra known as the Platonic solids. (Cube, Tetrahedron, and Octahedron are three of them).
