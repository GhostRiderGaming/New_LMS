📐 Definition

An angle is formed when two rays (or lines) share a common endpoint, called the vertex. The rays are referred to as the arms of the angle. It measures the amount of rotation required to map one arm onto the other.


The size of an angle is typically measured in degrees (°) or radians (rad).
