Right-Angled Triangle Lab

A polygon with three edges, three vertices, and exactly one internal angle equal to 90 degrees (π/2 radians). It is the foundation of trigonometry.

📚 Fundamentals

The sides adjacent to the right angle are the Base (a) and Perpendicular Height (b). The side opposite the right angle is the Hypotenuse (c), which is always the longest side.

💡 Fact: The ancient Babylonians knew the Pythagorean theorem 1,000 years before Pythagoras was born.
