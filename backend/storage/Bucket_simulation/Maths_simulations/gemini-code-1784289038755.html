Symmetry

Definition: Symmetry occurs when an object remains unchanged under a mathematical transformation, such as a reflection, rotation, or translation.

In this lab, we explore spatial transformations on a 2D Cartesian coordinate plane. A symmetrical shape can be divided into identical mirrored halves (reflection) or rotated to match its original outline.

Core Formulas

Reflection across Y-axis: P'(x', y') = (-x, y) Reflection across X-axis: P'(x', y') = (x, -y) Reflection across Origin (Point Symmetry): P'(x', y') = (-x, -y) Rotation by angle θ around origin: x' = x cos(θ) − y sin(θ) y' = x sin(θ) + y cos(θ)
💡 Did you know?

In physics, Noether's Theorem states that every continuous symmetry of the universe corresponds to a conserved quantity. For example, time symmetry leads to the conservation of energy!
