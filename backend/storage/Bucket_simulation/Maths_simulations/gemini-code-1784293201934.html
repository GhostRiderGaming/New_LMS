Angles of Elevation & Depression in Real-World Applications

Concepts

Line of Sight: The imaginary straight line drawn from the eye of an observer to the object being viewed.

Angle of Elevation: The angle formed by the line of sight and the horizontal plane when the object is above the horizontal level.

Angle of Depression: The angle formed by the line of sight and the horizontal plane when the object is below the horizontal level.

Did you know? Surveyors used these exact trigonometric principles in 1852 to calculate the height of Mount Everest from over 100 miles away!
