Linear Equation Laboratory

Definition

A linear equation in one variable is an algebraic equation in which the highest exponent of the variable is 1. It typically takes the form of a first-degree polynomial equating to a constant.

General Form

ax + b = c

Where a is the coefficient of x, b is a constant, and c is the result.

Did you know? Linear equations are the foundation of all computer graphics—every 3D shape is essentially a massive collection of linear intersections calculated by your GPU.
