All About Lines

A straight, one-dimensional figure extending infinitely in both directions.

In geometry, a line is uniquely defined by two distinct points. By analyzing the relationship between their x and y coordinates, we can determine the line's trajectory, steepness (slope), and formulate mathematical equations to predict any other point on that path.

Mathematical Formulas

Slope (m) = y₂ − y₁ x₂ − x₁
Distance (d) = √ (x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²
Midpoint (M) = ( x₁ + x₂ 2 , y₁ + y₂ 2 )

💡 Interesting Fact

In Euclidean (flat) space, a straight line is always the shortest distance between two points. However, on a curved surface like a sphere or the Earth, the shortest path is an arc called a Geodesic or Great Circle!

Also, a line with a slope of 0 is perfectly horizontal, while a perfectly vertical line has an undefined slope because division by zero is mathematically impossible.
