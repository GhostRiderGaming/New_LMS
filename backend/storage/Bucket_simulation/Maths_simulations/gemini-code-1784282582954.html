What is an Angle?

An angle is formed when two rays share a common endpoint, known as the vertex. It measures the amount of rotation required to bring one ray into coincidence with the other.

Mathematical Notation

θ = arccos( (a² + b² − c²) / 2ab )

Rad = Deg × (π / 180)

Fact: The term "angle" is derived from the Latin angulus, meaning "corner."
