🔍 Concept Definition

Area is the quantity that expresses the extent of a two-dimensional region or shape in the plane. It is measured in square units (e.g., m², cm²).


Think of it as the amount of paint required to cover a surface with a single coat.

📐 Essential Formulas

A = w × h
A = π r²
A = 12 b h
