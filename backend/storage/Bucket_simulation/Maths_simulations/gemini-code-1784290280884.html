Linear Equations Lab

Explore the interaction of equations in two variables

📚 Definition

A linear equation in two variables represents a straight line on a Cartesian coordinate plane. When two such equations are plotted together, they form a system of equations.

⚙️ Mechanics

By comparing two lines, we can determine their relationship. They can intersect at a single point (unique solution), run parallel (no solution), or overlap entirely (infinite solutions).
