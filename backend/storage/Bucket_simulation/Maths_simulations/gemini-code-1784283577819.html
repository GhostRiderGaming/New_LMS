Parameters & Perimeter of 2D Shapes

The Perimeter is the total length of the continuous line forming the boundary of a closed geometric figure. Parameters are the variables (like radius, width, or side length) that define the shape's size.

Adjust the parameters in the laboratory controls below to observe how the perimeter and area react in real-time. The tracer particle visually proves the perimeter length by traveling along the boundary.
