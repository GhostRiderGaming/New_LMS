Definition & Explanation

The midpoint is the exact middle point of a line segment connecting two arbitrary points in a coordinate plane. It divides the segment into two equal halves.

To find the midpoint visually or mathematically, you calculate the average of the X-coordinates and the average of the Y-coordinates of the two endpoints.

M = (
x1 + x2 2
,
y1 + y2 2
)
💡
Scientific Fact: René Descartes developed the Cartesian coordinate system in the 17th century, allegedly while lying in bed watching a fly crawl on the ceiling, realizing he could describe its exact position using two intersecting numerical axes.
