Trigonometric Ratios Laboratory

Definition: Trigonometric ratios define the mathematical relationship between the angles and lengths of sides in a right-angled triangle.

Imagine a point rotating around a circle. The position of that point can always be described by a right triangle formed with the axes. These relationships are fundamental to physics, engineering, graphics, and waveforms.

Random Fact 🎲: The word "sine" comes from a mistranslation! It originated from the Sanskrit word jya (meaning bowstring), which was transliterated into Arabic as jiba. Later scholars misread it as jaib (meaning bay or fold) and translated it into the Latin word sinus.
