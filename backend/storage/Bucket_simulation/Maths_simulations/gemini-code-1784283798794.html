Concept Overview

Curved Surface Area (CSA) is the area of all curved regions of a 3D solid, excluding its flat bases.

Imagine wrapping a label around a can. If you cut the label vertically and unroll it, it forms a rectangle. The area of this flat rectangle equals the curved surface area of the cylinder.

Cylinder CSA Equation
CSA = 2πrh
Where
2πr = Circumference of base
h = Height of cylinder
💡
Scientific Fact: Nature often minimizes surface area to conserve energy. A sphere has the smallest surface area for a given volume, which is why bubbles are round!
