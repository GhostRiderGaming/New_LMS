🔬 Definition

The Cartesian coordinate plane is formed by the intersection of a horizontal real number line (X-axis) and a vertical real number line (Y-axis). These axes divide the plane into four distinct regions called quadrants.

📖 Explanation

Quadrants are numbered counter-clockwise starting from the top right:

  • Quadrant I: (+x, +y)
  • Quadrant II: (-x, +y)
  • Quadrant III: (-x, -y)
  • Quadrant IV: (+x, -y)

💡 Interesting Fact

René Descartes invented the Cartesian coordinate system in the 17th century while lying in bed. He watched a fly crawling on his ceiling and realized he could mathematically describe the fly's exact position by measuring its distance from two adjacent walls.
