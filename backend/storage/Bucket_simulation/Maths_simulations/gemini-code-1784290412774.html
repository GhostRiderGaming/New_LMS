Trigonometry Laboratory

Concept Definition

Trigonometry is the branch of mathematics that studies relationships between side lengths and angles of triangles.


By mapping a right-angled triangle inside a circle (the Unit Circle), we can analyze cyclical phenomena like waves, oscillations, and rotations.
