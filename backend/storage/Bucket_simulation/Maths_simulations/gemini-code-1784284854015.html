All About Circles

A circle is a perfectly round 2D shape where every point on its edge is exactly the same distance from the center.

From planetary orbits to the wheels of a car, circles represent perfect mathematical symmetry. In geometry, the distance from the center to the edge is the radius, and the total distance around the edge is the circumference.

Area

A = πr2

Circumference

C = 2πr

Cartesian Equation

(x − h)2 + (y − k)2 = r2

Parametric Equations

x = h + r cos(θ)
y = k + r sin(θ)
💡

Scientific Fact

A circle is the most mathematically efficient shape in existence. For any given perimeter length, a circle will always encompass the absolute maximum possible area compared to any other polygon.
