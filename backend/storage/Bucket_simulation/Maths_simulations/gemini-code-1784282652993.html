📖 Definition

Two figures are similar if their corresponding angles are equal and the ratio of their corresponding side lengths is a constant value called the Scale Factor (k).

💡 Science Fact

The Square-Cube Law: Discovered by Galileo, this principle dictates that as a shape grows, its area scales by the square of the multiplier ($k^2$), but its volume scales by the cube ($k^3$). This is why ants can lift 50x their weight, but giant monsters (like Godzilla) would collapse under their own mass!
