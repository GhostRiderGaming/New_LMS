Trigonometric Identities

Interactive Visualization of the Pythagorean Identity

📚 Core Concept

A trigonometric identity is an equation that is always true for every valid angle.

sin2(θ) + cos2(θ) = 1

This is the fundamental Pythagorean Identity. It visually translates to: the square of the opposite side plus the square of the adjacent side equals the square of the hypotenuse (radius 1) on a unit circle.

tan(θ) = sin(θ) cos(θ)

💡 Did You Know?

The word "sine" comes from a historical mistranslation! Indian mathematician Aryabhata called it "jiva" (bowstring). Arabic translators wrote it as "jiba", which was later misread by Latin scholars as "jaib" (meaning fold or bay), leading to the Latin word "sinus".
