Definition & Properties

A quadrilateral is a two-dimensional closed shape with four straight sides, four vertices, and four interior angles.

The sum of the interior angles is always exactly 360° (in Euclidean geometry), assuming the shape is not self-intersecting (complex).

Squares are rectangles, and rectangles are parallelograms, but a parallelogram is not always a rectangle!

Key Formulas

Interior Angles Σθ = 360°
Trapezoid Area A = a + b 2 h

Side Length L = √(Δx)² + (Δy)²
