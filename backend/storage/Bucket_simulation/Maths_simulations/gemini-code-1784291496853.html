Introduction to Graphs

The Visual Language of Mathematics & Physics

A graph is a visual representation of relationships between variables on a Cartesian coordinate plane. By plotting points along the horizontal (X) and vertical (Y) axes, we translate abstract formulas into geometric curves, revealing trajectories, rates of change, and system behaviors.

Laboratory Fact

The Cartesian coordinate system was formalized by French philosopher and mathematician René Descartes in 1637. Legend says he invented it while lying in bed, watching a fly crawl on the ceiling, realizing he could describe its exact position using two intersecting lines!
