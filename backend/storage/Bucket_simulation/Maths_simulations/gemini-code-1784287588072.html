Basic Proportionality Theorem

Thales Theorem Interactive Laboratory

Definition

If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle intersecting the other two sides, then it divides the two sides in the same ratio.

Mathematical Formulation

In ΔABC, if a line DE is drawn parallel to BC such that it intersects AB at D and AC at E, then:

DE ∥ BC   &implies;  
AD DB
=
AE EC

Historical Fact

The Greek mathematician Thales of Miletus used this exact theorem over 2,500 years ago to calculate the height of the Egyptian Pyramids by measuring their shadows relative to a stick of known height!
