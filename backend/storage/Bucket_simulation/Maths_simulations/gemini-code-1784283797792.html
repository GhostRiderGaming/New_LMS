Charts & Their Representations

Visualizing numerical data through geometric mapping.

Visual Analytics

A chart represents tabular data visually, allowing the human brain to detect patterns, trends, and outliers instantly.

Important Formulas:
Mean:   x̄   =   Σxi n

Pie Sector:   θi   =   xi Σx   × 360°

Types of Representation

  • Bar Chart: Compares discrete categories.
  • Line Chart: Shows continuous trends over a sequence.
  • Pie Chart: Displays part-to-whole proportional relationships.
  • Scatter Plot: Reveals correlation between coordinates.

Data visualization leverages human visual processing (pre-attentive attributes) to parse large datasets in milliseconds.
