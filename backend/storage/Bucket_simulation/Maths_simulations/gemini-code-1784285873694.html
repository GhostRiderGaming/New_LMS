Definition & Theory

A segment of a circle is the region bounded by a chord and an arc. It is essentially a "slice" cut off from the main circle by a straight line.

To find the area of a segment, we calculate the area of the entire Sector (slice of pie) and subtract the area of the Triangle formed by the central angle and the chord.

💡
Scientific Fact: Fluids in horizontal cylindrical tanks (like fuel tankers) take the cross-sectional shape of a circular segment. Calculating the segment area is vital for knowing how much liquid is inside!
