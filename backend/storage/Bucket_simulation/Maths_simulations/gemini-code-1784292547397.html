The Pythagoras Theorem

Interactive geometric proof & real-time live calculation engine.

📐 Definition

In any right-angled triangle, the area of the square whose side is the hypotenuse is equal to the sum of the areas of the squares on the other two sides.

🧮 Important Formulas
a2 + b2 = c2
c = a2 + b2
💡 Scientific Fact

Although named after the Greek mathematician Pythagoras (c. 570–495 BC), Babylonian mathematicians knew this relationship over a thousand years earlier. The famous Plimpton 322 tablet contains a list of Pythagorean triples from 1800 BC!
