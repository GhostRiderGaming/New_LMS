Camera vs. Eye

Cameras focus by moving hard glass lenses closer or further from the sensor. The biological eye is much more advanced—it focuses by changing the actual shape (curvature) of a soft, jelly-like lens.

⚠️

Common Misconception

Many think muscles "squeeze" the lens to make it thick. Reality is opposite! Ciliary muscles form a ring. When they contract, the ring gets smaller, which relaxes the tension on ligaments. The lens thickens on its own due to natural elasticity.

👓

Presbyopia (Aging)

As we age (usually post-40), the lens loses its elasticity and hardens. Even though the muscles still contract, the lens refuses to bulge, making near vision blurry. This is why reading glasses become necessary.
