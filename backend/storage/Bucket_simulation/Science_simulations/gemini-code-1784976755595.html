Impact & Facts

The Heat Mistake

An incandescent bulb is essentially a space heater that happens to produce a little light. 90-95% of the energy you pay for is wasted as invisible infrared heat.

Beyond Lighting

This principle applies everywhere. Electric vehicles convert over 77% of electrical energy to the wheels. Gasoline engines convert only 12%–30% (the rest is heat and noise).

Cost Savings

Replacing one 60W incandescent with a 9W LED saves roughly $1.20 per month (if used 5hrs/day). Across a whole house over years, it's hundreds of dollars.
