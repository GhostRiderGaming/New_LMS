Crucial Distinction: Strength vs. Concentration

Strength is about the chemical nature (percentage of molecules that split). Concentration is about the amount of substance added to water.

You can have a highly concentrated weak acid (like strong vinegar), or a very dilute strong acid. A dilute strong acid might have a higher pH (less acidic) than a concentrated weak acid!

Biological Buffers

Your blood pH is strictly maintained between 7.35 and 7.45. How? Your body uses a mixture of a weak acid (carbonic acid) and its salt (bicarbonate). Because weak acids don't fully dissociate, they act as a sponge—absorbing excess H⁺ or OH⁻ to prevent dangerous pH swings.
