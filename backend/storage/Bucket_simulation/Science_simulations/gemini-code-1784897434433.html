Real-World Applications

Catalytic Converters

Cars use Platinum/Palladium as catalysts to force toxic gases (CO, NOx) to react into harmless N2 and CO2 before leaving the exhaust.

Digestion & Enzymes

Biological catalysts called enzymes allow complex food breakdown to happen at body temperature. Without them, digestion would take years.

⚡

Interesting Fact

Every 10°C increase in temperature approximately doubles the rate of most chemical reactions. This is why we use refrigerators to slow down food spoilage (which is a chemical reaction).

⚠️

Common Mistake

Thinking catalysts are consumed in a reaction. They are not! A catalyst participates in the transition state but emerges entirely unchanged at the end, ready to be used again.

Quick Revision

  • Collision Theory: Particles must physically collide with correct orientation and sufficient energy to react.
  • Activation Energy (Ea): The minimum energy barrier required to break initial bonds.
  • Temperature: Increases particle velocity, leading to more frequent and harder collisions.
  • Concentration: Crowds the space, leading to more frequent collisions.
  • Catalyst: Provides an alternative pathway with a lower Activation Energy, speeding up the reaction without being consumed.
