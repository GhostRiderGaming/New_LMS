Interesting Fact

What is MPGe?

Miles Per Gallon Equivalent (MPGe) is a metric created by the EPA. It calculates how far a vehicle can travel on 33.7 kilowatt-hours (kWh) of electricity—which is the exact amount of heat energy contained in one gallon of gasoline. Because EVs waste so little energy as heat, they routinely achieve over 100 MPGe.

Common Mistake

"Idling uses zero fuel"

An internal combustion engine must continuously burn fuel just to overcome internal friction and keep running, even at zero RPMs. This is 0% efficient. Electric vehicles use practically zero energy when stopped, giving them a massive efficiency advantage in stop-and-go city traffic.

Quick Revision
