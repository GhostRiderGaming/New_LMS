Mechanism

Quantum Leaps (Absorption & Emission)

How an atom interacts with light. Electrons cannot exist between shells. They must instantly "leap" by absorbing or emitting an exact packet of energy (photon).

1. Ground State

The electron occupies the lowest available energy level (n=1). The atom is stable.

Model Limitations

The Bohr model is highly accurate for Hydrogen, but fails to predict spectral lines of larger atoms. It incorrectly assumes electrons travel in flat, perfectly circular 2D orbits rather than 3D probability clouds (orbitals).

Real World App: Neon

Neon lights work purely on this principle. Electricity excites electrons in Neon gas to higher shells. When they instantly fall back, they emit specific wavelengths of light, creating the iconic red-orange glow.
