Why Use Vegetative Propagation?

Genetic Clones

Produces exact genetic copies of the parent. Ideal for preserving specific desirable traits like fruit sweetness or flower color across generations.

Faster Growth

Bypasses the vulnerable seedling stage. A stem cutting will mature and bear fruit much faster than a plant grown from a seed.

Seedless Varieties

It is the only way to commercialize seedless plants (like bananas or seedless grapes) since they cannot reproduce sexually.

Common Mistakes

Planting Upside Down

Cuttings have polarity. If planted upside down, the roots and shoots will try to grow in the wrong directions, usually resulting in failure.

No Node Present

Roots and shoots develop from meristematic tissue found at nodes. A cutting taken from an internode (space between nodes) will rot, not root.
