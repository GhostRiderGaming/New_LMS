The Molecular Balance

At the heart of the pH scale is water ($H_2O$). A tiny fraction of water molecules constantly split apart into two highly reactive ions:

Hydrogen ions (H⁺) and Hydroxide ions (OH⁻).

The term "pH" stands for 'potential of Hydrogen'. It is a mathematical scale used to specify the acidity or basicity of an aqueous solution based on the concentration of these H⁺ ions.

The Logarithmic Secret

The pH scale is logarithmic (Base 10). This means a change of 1 whole number represents a 10x change in concentration.

A pH of 4 is 10 times more acidic than pH 5, and 100 times more acidic than pH 6!

🩸

Real World: Your Blood

Human blood must stay in a very narrow range: pH 7.35 to 7.45 (slightly basic). If it drops below 7.0 or rises above 7.8, it can be fatal.

Your kidneys and lungs constantly work to maintain this balance.

❌

Common Mistake

"Neutral means there are no H+ or OH- ions."

False! Neutral (pH 7) means the number of H+ ions is exactly equal to the number of OH- ions, cancelling each other out.
