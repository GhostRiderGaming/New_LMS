Interesting Physics Facts

  • Glass is a liquid? False. It's an amorphous solid. Its molecules are locked in place, but randomly arranged, allowing light to slip through the gaps.
  • Deep water is opaque. Even though water is transparent, it absorbs a tiny bit of light. Below 1,000 meters in the ocean, it's completely dark.
  • Metals reflect, not just absorb. Metals are opaque because their free-flowing electrons immediately interact with light waves, bouncing them back (reflection).
⚠️

Common Misconceptions

  • Mistake: Thinking translucent objects block half the light.
    Truth: They often transmit most of the light, but they scatter the direction of the photons, destroying the image.
  • Mistake: Believing shadows are physical things.
    Truth: A shadow is merely the absence of light where an opaque object has absorbed or reflected the photons.
