The Core Principle

The "Sea of Electrons"

Unlike other materials, metal atoms don't hold tightly to their outer electrons. They form a rigid lattice of positive ions swimming in a fluid, continuous "sea" of highly mobile electrons. This single structure explains almost every metallic property.

Common Misconceptions

  • Myth: Electrons flow at the speed of light in a wire.
    Fact: The signal travels near light-speed, but individual electrons drift very slowly (drift velocity is ~0.1 mm/second).
  • Myth: All metals are solid at room temperature.
    Fact: Mercury (Hg) is a liquid, due to relativistic effects contracting its s-orbital electrons.
