The Principle of Separation

To separate a mixture, we must find a physical property where the components differ. The greater the difference, the easier the separation.

Size Difference

When particles vary in physical dimensions.

Technique: Filtration, Sieving

Boiling Point

When substances vaporize at different temperatures.

Technique: Distillation, Evaporation

Density & Magnetism

Differences in mass per volume or magnetic response.

Technique: Decantation, Magnetic Separation

Interesting Fact

Chromatography, used today in forensic DNA analysis, was originally invented by a botanist to separate plant pigments, deriving its name from Greek words meaning "color writing".

Common Mistake

Thinking filtration can separate dissolved salt from water. Since the salt ions are smaller than the filter pores, they pass right through. Evaporation or distillation must be used.
