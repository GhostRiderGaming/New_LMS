Wind is born from inequality. It is nature's attempt to balance temperature and pressure differences created by the Sun.

⚡

Real-World Application

Modern wind turbines harness kinetic energy from wind. They are strategically placed in coastal areas or mountain gaps where temperature gradients create reliable, strong winds.

❌

Common Misconception

Many think wind is just "air blowing." It's actually a pressure equalization process. Wind doesn't push; High Pressure air rushes in to fill the void left by rising Low Pressure air.

Quick Revision

  • Source: All wind begins with the Sun unevenly heating the Earth's surface.
  • Low Pressure: Warm air expands, becomes less dense, and rises.
  • High Pressure: Cool air condenses, becomes denser, and sinks.
  • The Flow: Air always moves from High Pressure areas to Low Pressure areas.
  • Coriolis Effect: Earth's rotation curves the path of wind, preventing it from flowing in straight lines.
