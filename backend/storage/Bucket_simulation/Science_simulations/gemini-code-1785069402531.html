Real-world Impact: Tonicity in Blood Cells

Osmosis isn't just theory. It dictates whether our red blood cells survive or die based on the fluid surrounding them (tonicity).

Hypertonic Solution
Outside fluid has MORE solute than cell.
Result: Water leaves. Cell shrivels (Crenation).
Isotonic Solution
Equal solute concentration.
Result: Water flows equally. Normal shape.
Hypotonic Solution
Outside fluid has LESS solute than cell.
Result: Water rushes in. Cell swells and may burst (Lysis).

Key Takeaways

!
Core Principle

Nature hates a vacuum, and it also hates a gradient. Both diffusion and osmosis are passive attempts by nature to achieve equilibrium (balance) without spending energy (ATP).

✕
Common Mistake

People often say "Water moves from low to high concentration in osmosis." This is confusing. Water moves from high WATER concentration (low solute) to low WATER concentration (high solute).
