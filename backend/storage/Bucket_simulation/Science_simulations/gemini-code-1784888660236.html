Real-World Applications

Kidney Function

Human kidneys contain millions of microscopic filters called nephrons. They filter blood, retaining essential proteins and cells while passing urea and excess water as urine.

Air Purification (HEPA)

High-Efficiency Particulate Air filters force air through a fine mesh of fiberglass. They trap harmful particles like pollen, pet dander, dust mites, and smoke down to 0.3 microns.

Water Treatment

Municipal water passes through layers of sand, gravel, and charcoal. This macroscopic filtration removes large debris, dirt, and parasites before chemical disinfection.
