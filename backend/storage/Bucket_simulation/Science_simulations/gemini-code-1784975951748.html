Toxic Chain

Biomagnification is the hidden process where toxic chemicals, like microplastics or heavy metals, become increasingly concentrated as they move up the food chain. What starts as a harmless trace in the water ends up as a lethal dose in apex predators.

Key Takeaways

⚠️

Bioaccumulation vs. Biomagnification

Common Mistake: Confusing the two.

Bioaccumulation happens within a single organism over its lifetime.
Biomagnification happens across different trophic levels in a food chain.

🦅

The Silent Spring Effect

The pesticide DDT magnified in aquatic food chains, causing birds of prey (like Bald Eagles) to lay eggs with shells so thin they broke during incubation. Banning DDT allowed their populations to recover.

🍣

Why pregnant women limit Tuna

Mercury from industrial pollution falls into oceans, converts to methylmercury, and biomagnifies. Large, long-lived predators like Tuna and Swordfish have the highest concentrations, which can affect fetal brain development.
