Interesting Fact

Gregor Mendel discovered the laws of inheritance, but he never used a Punnett Square! It was invented in 1905 by Reginald Punnett to visually simplify Mendel's complex mathematical probabilities.

Common Mistake

Confusing Genotype (the actual letters/genes, e.g., 'Yy') with Phenotype (how it looks, e.g., 'Yellow'). Two different genotypes (YY and Yy) can produce the exact same visible phenotype.

Quick Revision

  • Capital letter = Dominant allele.
  • Lowercase letter = Recessive allele.
  • Homozygous = Same letters (YY, yy).
  • Heterozygous = Different letters (Yy).
  • The square shows probability, not exact offspring counts.
