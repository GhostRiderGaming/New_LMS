What is Free Fall?

In physics, free fall is any motion of a body where gravity is the only force acting upon it. In a strict sense, this means the object is moving in a vacuum, completely free of air resistance or friction.

Under these conditions, a feather and a bowling ball dropped from the same height will hit the ground at the exact same time.

  • 1

    Mass ($m$)

    The amount of matter. In a vacuum, mass does not affect the rate of fall.

  • 2

    Gravity Force ($F_g$)

    Downward pull creating constant acceleration ($9.8 m/s^2$ on Earth).

  • 3

    Velocity ($v$)

    Speed in a given direction. Increases linearly over time: $v = g \cdot t$.

  • 4

    Air Resistance ($F_d$)

    Opposing force in an atmosphere. Grows with square of velocity ($v^2$).

  • 5

    Displacement ($h$)

    Distance fallen. Increases quadratically: $h = \frac{1}{2}gt^2$.

Beyond the Textbooks

Orbit is Free Fall

The International Space Station isn't in "zero gravity". It is falling towards Earth, but moving sideways so fast that the Earth curves away beneath it. It falls constantly, missing the ground.

Terminal Velocity

In air, an object accelerates until the upward drag force equals the downward gravity force. At this point, net force is zero, and it falls at a constant, maximum speed.

Apollo 15 Experiment

In 1971, astronaut David Scott dropped a geological hammer and a falcon feather simultaneously on the Moon. In the lunar vacuum, they hit the dirt at the exact same time.

Common Misconception

"Heavier objects fall faster."

False. Aristotle thought this, but Galileo proved him wrong. Gravity pulls harder on a heavier object ($F=mg$), but a heavier object also has more inertia (resistance to motion). These two effects perfectly cancel out ($a = F/m = mg/m = g$). All masses fall at the same rate in a vacuum.

Quick Revision

  • Definition: Motion under gravity alone.
  • Acceleration ($g$): Constant $9.8 m/s^2$ downwards (on Earth).
  • Velocity: Increases by $9.8 m/s$ every second ($v=gt$).
  • Distance: Proportional to time squared ($h=\frac{1}{2}gt^2$).
  • Air Resistance: The only reason feathers fall slower than rocks on Earth.
