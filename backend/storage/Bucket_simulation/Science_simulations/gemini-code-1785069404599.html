4. Key Insights

Girth Growth (Secondary)

Trees grow wider because of the Vascular Cambium, a thin layer of meristematic tissue between xylem and phloem that constantly produces new cells, creating tree rings.

Common Misconception

People think plants "drink" water using energy. In reality, water evaporates from leaves (transpiration), creating a physical vacuum that pulls water up the xylem passively.

Wood vs. Bark

What we call "Wood" is actually just years of accumulated, dead Secondary Xylem. The inner bark contains the living Phloem. Girdling (cutting bark around a tree) kills it by severing the phloem.
