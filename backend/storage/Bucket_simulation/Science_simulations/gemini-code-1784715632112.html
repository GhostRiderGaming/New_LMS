4. Key Takeaways & Facts

Bioaccumulation

Heavy metals from industry don't just stay in water; they build up in the tissues of small fish, and magnify in predators (like humans) at the top of the food chain.

The "Dead Zone"

The Gulf of Mexico has a hypoxic "Dead Zone" the size of New Jersey, caused primarily by agricultural runoff traveling down the Mississippi River.

Thermal Pollution

Warm water holds less dissolved oxygen than cold water. Industrial cooling pipes discharging warm water can suffocate fish even without adding chemicals.
