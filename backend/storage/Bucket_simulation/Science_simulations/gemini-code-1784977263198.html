Real World Context

💧

True Solutions

Examples: Saltwater, Air, Brass.

Fact: Medical IV fluids must be true solutions to enter the bloodstream without causing blockages. Particles are reduced to individual ions.

🥛

Colloids

Examples: Milk, Fog, Blood, Gelatin.

Fact: Colloidal particles are kept suspended by constant microscopic collisions with solvent molecules—a phenomenon known as Brownian Motion.

☕

Suspensions

Examples: Muddy water, Liquid medicines (antibiotics), Freshly squeezed orange juice.

Fact: "Shake well before use" labels exist specifically for suspensions, as the active ingredients sink to the bottom over time.
