How do atoms actually build up? Electrons fill orbitals starting from the lowest available energy state before moving to higher ones.

Why this matters

Chemical Bonding

Only the outermost (valence) electrons interact. The unfilled shells drive elements to react, share, or steal electrons to reach stability.

🌈

Colors & Lasers

When electrons jump between these specific energy shells, they absorb or emit exact wavelengths of light, giving us neon signs and lasers.

Common Misconceptions

❌

"Electrons are tiny planets"

Unlike planets in fixed orbits, electrons exist in 3D "probability clouds" (orbitals). We only know where they probably are.

🔢

The 3d anomaly

Logic suggests 3d fills before 4s. However, the 4s orbital actually has slightly lower energy than 3d, so it fills first (Potassium/Calcium) before 3d begins filling (Scandium).
