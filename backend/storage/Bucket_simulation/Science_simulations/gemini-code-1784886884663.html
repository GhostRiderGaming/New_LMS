Critical Botany Insights

★ Mobility Dictates Symptoms

Why do older leaves turn yellow first in Nitrogen deficiency? Nitrogen is a "mobile" nutrient. The plant sacrifices older leaves, moving the nitrogen up to ensure new growth survives.

⚠ The pH Lockout

Adding fertilizer doesn't always fix the issue. If soil pH is too high or low, nutrients become chemically bound and unavailable to roots, creating a "lockout" deficiency even in rich soil.

⟳ Quick Diagnosis Key

• Old Leaves Yellow: Nitrogen (N)
• Old Leaves Purple: Phosphorus (P)
• Old Leaves Brown Edges: Potassium (K)
• New Leaves Distorted/Dead: Calcium (Ca)
• New Leaves Yellow (green veins): Iron (Fe)
