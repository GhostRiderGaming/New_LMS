    Optical Center (O)

    The exact center of the lens. Rays passing through here do not deviate.

  • 3

    Focal Point (F)

    Point where parallel light rays converge after passing through the lens.

  • 4

    Convex Lens

    A transparent medium shaped to bend light inwards (converge).

  • 5

    Formed Image

    The visual reproduction formed where refracted light rays intersect.

  • 6

    Principal Axis

    An imaginary straight line passing through the center of curvature.

Real-World Applications

👁️

The Human Eye

Your cornea and crystalline lens act as a convex system, projecting an inverted real image onto the retina. The brain seamlessly flips this image upright.

📷

Digital Cameras

Cameras use complex arrays of lenses to adjust focal length (zoom) and ensure parallel convergence precisely onto the CMOS or CCD sensor.

🔬

Microscopes

Utilizing multiple lenses in sequence, microscopes drastically increase magnification ($m_1 \times m_2$) to form heavily enlarged virtual images of tiny objects.
