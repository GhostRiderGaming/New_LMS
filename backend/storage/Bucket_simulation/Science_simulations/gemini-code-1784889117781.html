Beyond the Basics

🧠 Fascinating Fact

Some roots can secrete chemicals to "talk" to fungi in the soil, trading sugars they made in the leaves for hard-to-reach minerals gathered by the fungal network (Mycorrhizae).

⚠️ Common Mistake

"Plants eat soil." False! Plants build their mass primarily from Carbon Dioxide in the air, not the soil. Soil provides water and trace minerals, but the structural carbon comes from thin air.

💡 Application

Understanding xylem transport allows arborists to inject medicines directly into tree trunks to fight pests. The tree's own plumbing carries the medicine to every leaf automatically.
