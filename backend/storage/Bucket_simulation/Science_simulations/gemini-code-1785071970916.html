★ Fascinating Fact

Legumes & Nitrogen: Plants like peas and beans form a symbiotic relationship with Rhizobium bacteria in their root nodules. The bacteria pull nitrogen directly from the air and convert it into a form the plant can use, acting as natural, built-in fertilizer.

⚙ Modern Application

Precision Agriculture: Today, drones and satellites use multispectral imaging to measure the exact chlorophyll levels in individual fields. Tractors equipped with GPS then apply fertilizer *only* to specific square meters that need it, saving resources.

⚠ Common Mistake

Over-irrigation: More water isn't always better. Flooding soil removes oxygen spaces between soil particles. Without oxygen, roots cannot undergo cellular respiration, effectively causing the plant to "drown" and roots to rot.
