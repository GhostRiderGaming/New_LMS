Historical Evolution

Before machines, humans used flails (wooden sticks jointed together) to manually beat harvested crops on a threshing floor. It took days to do what modern machines do in minutes.

🌪️

Aerodynamic Winnowing

Threshing only detaches the grain. It's mixed with chaff (husks). The next step is always winnowing—using directed air flow to blow away the lighter chaff while heavy grain falls straight down.

🧬

Crop Variables

There is no "one setting fits all". Wheat requires higher cylinder speeds than delicate crops like soybeans. Moisture content also drastically changes the required concave clearance.
