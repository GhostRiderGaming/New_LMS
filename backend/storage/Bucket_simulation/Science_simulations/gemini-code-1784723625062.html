Physical vs Chemical Changes

Explore the fundamental mechanisms that alter the universe, from freezing water to molecular synthesis.

Physical
Chemical

State Alteration

The substance changes form, shape, or state (solid, liquid, gas), but its molecular identity remains exactly the same. Usually reversible.

Molecular Transformation

Chemical bonds are broken and new ones are formed, creating an entirely new substance with different properties. Usually irreversible.

Reaction Vessel (Flask)

Thermometer

Measures energy change. Exothermic (heat released) or endothermic (heat absorbed) reactions indicate chemical changes.

Heat Source (Bunsen Burner)

Fuel Supply

💎

Intriguing Fact

Making a diamond from coal is a physical change. Both are pure Carbon; the atoms just rearrange from a loose lattice into a highly pressurized, rigid structure.

🤔

Common Misconception

Many think dissolving sugar in water is a chemical change because the sugar "disappears". It's a physical change; the sugar molecules just spread out and can be recovered by evaporating the water.

⚡

Quick Identification

If you see unexpected color changes, smell a new odor, see light/heat produced without an external source, or see a solid form from two liquids—it's almost certainly a chemical change.
