Real World Connections

☕

Brewing Coffee

Hot water extracts flavor compounds much faster and more efficiently than cold water due to increased solubility.

🌊

Ocean Salinity

Oceans are giant solutions. Rain washes minerals (solutes) from land into the sea, where they dissolve in water.

⚠️

Melting vs. Dissolving

Mistake: Thinking sugar "melts" in tea.
Truth: Melting requires heat to change a solid to liquid. Dissolving requires a solvent to break bonds.

⚠️

"It disappeared!"

Mistake: Thinking dissolved matter vanishes.
Truth: The law of conservation of mass applies. If you boil off the water, the solid remains.
