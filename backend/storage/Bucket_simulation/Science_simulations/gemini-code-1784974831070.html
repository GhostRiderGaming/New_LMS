Ohm's Law

The Fundamental Equation of Electricity

V = I × R

The Electrical Trinity

Just like water flowing through pipes, electricity is governed by three invisible forces. Ohm's Law reveals how they perfectly balance each other.

Real World Application

Incandescent Bulbs: The filament has high resistance. When voltage is applied, the restriction causes friction among flowing electrons, generating intense heat and light.

🧠

Interesting Fact

Georg Ohm published his law in 1827, but it was initially rejected by colleagues who called it a "web of naked fancies." It took 14 years for his genius to be recognized.

⚠️

Common Mistake

Thinking a battery supplies constant Current (A). It actually supplies constant Voltage (V). The current drawn depends entirely on what you connect to it (Resistance).
