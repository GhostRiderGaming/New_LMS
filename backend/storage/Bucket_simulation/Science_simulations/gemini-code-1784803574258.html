Critical Insights

The First Flush Rule

The first 10 minutes of rain wash pollutants off the roof. Modern systems use a "first flush diverter" to discard this water before it enters the pristine storage tank.

Aquifer Recharge

Allowing overflow to seep into a soak pit doesn't just prevent flooding; it recharges local groundwater tables, combatting soil subsidence and drought.

Transparent Tanks

A common mistake is storing water in translucent containers. Sunlight exposure promotes rapid algae growth, turning the water green and toxic. Tanks must be opaque.
