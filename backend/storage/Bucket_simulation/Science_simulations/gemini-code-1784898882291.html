Fascinating Facts & Misconceptions

🌎

100,000 Miles

If you laid all your blood vessels end to end, they would wrap around the Earth roughly four times.

❌
Myth: Blood in veins is blue Fact: It's dark red

Human blood is never blue. Deoxygenated blood is just a darker shade of red. Veins appear blue through the skin due to light scattering physics (optical illusion).

⚡

Own Electricity

The heart has its own electrical system (the SA node) and can continue to beat outside the body as long as it has oxygen.
