Scientific Facts

⚡ ATP Production

One single molecule of glucose yields up to 38 molecules of ATP (cellular energy currency) during cellular respiration.

🧠 Brain Fat

Nearly 60% of the human brain is composed of fat, making healthy dietary lipids critical for cognitive function.

Reality Check

"Carbs make you gain fat immediately."

Excess total calories cause fat storage. Carbs are primarily stored as glycogen in muscles for immediate energy use.
