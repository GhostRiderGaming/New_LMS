Interesting Fact

The stomach produces a new layer of mucus every 2 weeks. Without this protective barrier, the hydrochloric acid would digest the stomach itself.

Common Misconception

Gravity doesn't move food down. You can eat while hanging upside down because muscles in the esophagus push food via peristalsis.

Surface Area

The small intestine contains millions of finger-like projections called villi. Unfolded, its surface area is roughly the size of a tennis court.
