    Trees provide timber, fruit, and fodder, while fixing nitrogen in the soil.

Real-World Context

💧

Water Conservation

IFS can reduce freshwater usage by up to 60% because pond water is reused for irrigation, and soil moisture is retained by agroforestry shading.

⚡

Energy Independence

A family farm with just 4-5 cows can generate enough biogas to completely replace LPG for daily cooking and run a small electricity generator.

⚠️

Common Mistake: Overstocking

Adding too many animals without increasing crop/pond area leads to nitrogen leaching. The soil cannot absorb the excess manure, polluting local groundwater.
