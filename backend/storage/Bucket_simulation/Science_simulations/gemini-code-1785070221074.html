Real-world Rarity

True uniform motion is extremely rare on Earth due to friction and air resistance. It usually requires artificial environments or deep space.

The Direction Trap

An object moving at a steady 50mph in a circle is in non-uniform motion. Why? Because velocity includes direction, and the direction is constantly changing, meaning it is accelerating.
