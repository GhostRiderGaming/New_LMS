🤿 Real-World Application

The Diving Bell

The principle demonstrated here is exactly how early diving bells worked. A heavy, open-bottomed chamber is lowered into the ocean. The air trapped inside cannot escape, creating a dry pocket where humans can breathe underwater.

Did you know? Diving spiders (Argyroneta aquatica) use this exact physics. They weave a web, trap a bubble of air at the surface, and drag it down to create an underwater home.

Common Mistake

Thinking the glass is "empty". It's full of a fluid (air). Two fluids cannot occupy the same space simultaneously unless one compresses or is displaced.

Quick Revision

1. Air is matter (mass + volume).
2. In a straight plunge, air pressure balances water pressure.
3. Tilting breaks the seal, air escapes (bubbles), water replaces it.
