Conductors vs Insulators

System Components

Thermal Source

Provides continuous thermal energy (heat). Molecules here possess high kinetic energy.

2

Conductor (Metal)

Heat flows rapidly. Free electrons carry kinetic energy swiftly across the material structure.

3

Insulator (Wood)

Heat flow is blocked. Lacking free electrons, energy transfers slowly only through localized atom bumping.

Practical Engineering

How we utilize both properties in everyday object design.

Cookware

Frying pans utilize a metal body (conductor) to transfer stove heat quickly to food, paired with a plastic/wood handle (insulator) to protect human hands.

Double-Glazed Windows

Trapping a layer of Argon gas or air (excellent insulators) between two glass panes drastically reduces heat loss from buildings in winter.

Heat Sinks

Computers use copper or aluminum fins attached to processors. Their high thermal conductivity pulls heat away from chips to prevent melting.
