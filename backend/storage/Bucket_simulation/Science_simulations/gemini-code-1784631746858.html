Why It Matters

Engine Cylinders (Cross-hatching)

They are intentionally made rough (Ra ~1µm) with a cross-hatch pattern so microscopic valleys hold lubricating oil. If perfectly smooth, engines would seize.

Medical Implants

Hip joint replacements need a mirror finish (Ra < 0.05µm) to prevent wear, while the bone-anchoring side is made highly rough to encourage bone tissue ingrowth.

Aerospace Hydraulics

Seals require specific roughness. Too smooth, and the seal scrapes off all fluid (dry friction). Too rough, and fluid leaks through the deep valleys.
