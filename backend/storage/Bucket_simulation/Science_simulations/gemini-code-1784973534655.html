The Blue Eye Mutation

Originally, all humans had brown eyes. A genetic mutation affecting the OCA2 gene arose in a single individual near the Black Sea 6,000–10,000 years ago, turning off the ability to produce brown melanin in the iris.

🧬

Not Always Black & White

Many human traits are Polygenic (controlled by multiple genes working together), like height, skin color, and intelligence. Simple dominant/recessive inheritance is mostly found in single-gene traits.

⚠️

Common Misconception

"Dominant" does NOT mean "most common in the population." It only refers to how alleles interact in an individual. For example, having five fingers is a recessive trait, but having six (Polydactyly) is dominant!
