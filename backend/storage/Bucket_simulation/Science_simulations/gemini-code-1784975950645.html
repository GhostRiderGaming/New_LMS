Organic Matter

Decomposers (Microbes)

Bacteria and fungi that secrete enzymes to break chemical bonds in organic matter.

3

Synthetic Material

Man-made plastics (like PET). The molecular structure does not exist in the natural food chain.

4

Unbreakable Bonds

Strong carbon-carbon bonds. Microbes lack the specific enzymes required to break these polymers apart.

💡

Interesting Fact

A glass bottle can take over 1 million years to decompose in the environment. However, glass is 100% indefinitely recyclable without loss in quality or purity.

⚠️

Common Mistake

Thinking "Biodegradable Plastic" means you can throw it in nature. Most require industrial composting facilities with high heat (140°F/60°C) to break down, otherwise they act like regular plastic.
