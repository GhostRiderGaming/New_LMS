Interactive Logic: If fuel > air, combustion is incomplete (yellow flame, soot). If air ≥ fuel, combustion is complete (blue, hot flame).
🔵

Complete Combustion

Sufficient oxygen supply.

  • Blue, non-luminous flame
  • Very high temperature
  • Produces CO₂ and H₂O only
  • No soot (clean burn)
🔥

Incomplete Combustion

Insufficient oxygen supply.

  • Yellow, luminous flame
  • Lower temperature
  • Produces Carbon Monoxide (CO)
  • Produces Carbon particles (soot)
