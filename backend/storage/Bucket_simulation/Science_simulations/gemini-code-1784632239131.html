The Experimental Setup

To test a liquid, we need a complete circuit. Hover over or tap the markers to understand the apparatus.

1

Power Source (Battery)

Provides the electrical push (Voltage) needed to move charges through the circuit.

2

Conducting Wires

Copper paths that carry negatively charged electrons between components.

3

Indicator (LED)

Lights up if the circuit is complete. Brightness indicates the conductivity level.

4

Electrodes

Solid conductors (like graphite) dipped into the liquid to transfer electrons in and out.

5

Test Liquid (Electrolyte)

The substance being tested. It must contain free-moving ions to complete the circuit.

Applications & Facts

Electroplating

The movement of ions in liquids is used to coat cheap metals with expensive ones (like gold-plated jewelry). The object to be plated acts as an electrode.

Human Body

Your body conducts electricity because it is roughly 60% water filled with dissolved salts (electrolytes). This is how our nervous system sends signals!

Common Mistake

Many think "Water conducts electricity". Actually, 100% pure distilled water is an insulator! It's the dissolved minerals and impurities in tap water that conduct the charge.
