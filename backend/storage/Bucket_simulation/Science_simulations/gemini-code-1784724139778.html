Biomimicry

Engineers study biological adaptations to solve human problems.

Shinkansen Bullet Train

Early Japanese bullet trains created sonic booms when exiting tunnels. Engineers adapted the nose design of the Kingfisher bird (which dives into water without a splash) to make the train faster, quieter, and 15% more efficient.

Velcro

Invented in 1941 by a Swiss engineer who noticed how Burdock burrs continuously stuck to his dog's fur. Under a microscope, he saw tiny hooks that caught on loops of hair, inspiring hook-and-loop fasteners.

Facts & Misconceptions

💡
Fact: Convergent Evolution
Sharks (fish) and Dolphins (mammals) have almost identical streamlined body shapes and dorsal fins, not because they are related, but because they adapted to the exact same aquatic environment.
❌
Common Mistake
"Individuals adapt to their environment."
Correction: Individuals acclimate (like getting a tan). Only populations adapt over multiple generations through genetic inheritance. A giraffe cannot "try" to grow a longer neck in its lifetime.

Quick Revision

  • Adaptation: A heritable trait that helps an organism survive and reproduce in its present environment.
  • Natural Selection: The mechanism driving adaptation. Individuals with favorable traits survive longer and pass those traits to offspring.
  • Structural: Physical body parts (e.g., thick fur, large ears).
  • Physiological: Internal processes (e.g., countercurrent heat exchange, venom production).
  • Behavioral: Actions organisms take (e.g., migrating, burrowing during heat).
