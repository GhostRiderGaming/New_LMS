Real-World Impact

Economic Value

A single aluminum can recycled saves 95% of the energy required to make a new one from bauxite ore. Segregation preserves this material value.

Methane Reduction

When organic waste goes to landfill, it decomposes anaerobically, releasing methane—a greenhouse gas 25x more potent than CO2. Composting prevents this.

The Contamination Risk

Just one leaky battery or food-soiled container can ruin an entire ton of recyclable paper or plastic, forcing it to be sent to a landfill.
