DC Power Supply

Provides the electron "push" (voltage). Must be Direct Current (DC) to maintain consistent polarity.

2

Anode (+)

The pure source metal (e.g., Copper). It oxidizes, dissolving into the solution as positive ions.

3

Cathode (-)

The object to be plated. It receives electrons, attracting positive metal ions which turn into solid metal on its surface.

4

Electrolyte

A conductive liquid containing dissolved salts of the plating metal, allowing ion transport.

Faraday's Laws

The mass of the substance deposited is directly proportional to the total electrical charge passed through the electrolyte. (More current + more time = thicker plating).

Reversing Polarity

Connecting the target object to the positive terminal turns it into the anode. Instead of getting plated, your valuable object will dissolve into the acid!

Real Applications

Used to prevent rust (galvanization), purify metals (refining copper to 99.99% for wiring), and create beautiful jewelry (gold/silver plating).
