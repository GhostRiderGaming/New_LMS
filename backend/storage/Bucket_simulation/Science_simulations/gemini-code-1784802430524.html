Primary Driver

Surprisingly, your body doesn't primarily breathe to get oxygen. The strongest trigger to breathe is actually the accumulation of Carbon Dioxide (CO2) in your blood, which lowers blood pH.

⚠️
Common Mistake

Many believe the lungs have muscles to pull themselves open. In reality, lungs are passive. The diaphragm and intercostal muscles do the work, creating a vacuum that air rushes in to fill.

🔬
Gas Exchange

Within the lungs are roughly 600 million tiny air sacs called alveoli. If flattened out, their surface area would cover an entire tennis court, maximizing the speed of gas exchange.
