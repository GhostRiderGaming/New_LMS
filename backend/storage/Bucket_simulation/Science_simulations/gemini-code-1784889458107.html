Living Tissue

Bones are actively living organs. Every 10 years, your skeleton completely replaces itself through cellular remodeling (osteoblasts building, osteoclasts breaking down).

🩸

The Blood Factory

The marrow inside your long bones (like the femur) acts as a factory, producing over 200 billion red blood cells every single day.

✕

Common Misconception

People think bones are the hardest substance in the body. They aren't. Tooth enamel is much harder and is the only part that cannot heal itself.
