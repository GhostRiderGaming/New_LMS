Beyond the Basics

Real-World Application

CRISPR Gene Editing: Scientists can now act like biological word processors, finding specific flawed alleles causing diseases and replacing them with healthy versions, forever changing the inheritance line.

Fascinating Fact

Humans share about 50% of their DNA with bananas! This doesn't mean we are half banana; it means the fundamental housekeeping genes for cell survival evolved early and haven't changed much.

Common Mistake

"Dominant means stronger/better." False. Dominant just means it masks the recessive allele if both are present. Having extra fingers (Polydactyly) is a dominant trait, but it's rare in the population.
