The Invisible Threat: Step Voltage

When lightning hits the ground, massive current spreads outward in concentric rings of decreasing voltage. If your feet are apart, they cross different voltage rings.

This creates a potential difference, allowing current to flow up one leg, through your core, and down the other.

5,000 V
Survival Posture: Keep feet strictly together. Hop if you must move. This keeps both feet on the same voltage ring (0 potential difference).

The 30-30 Rule Tracker

Light travels instantly (~300,000 km/s). Sound is slow (~0.34 km/s). By counting the seconds between the flash and the thunder, you can calculate distance.

Energy Output

A single stroke carries up to 300 million volts and 30,000 amps. It heats the surrounding air to 30,000°C (54,000°F) — five times hotter than the surface of the sun.

🚫

Common Myth

Rubber shoes do NOT protect you. The voltage required to jump miles through the air will instantly bypass half an inch of rubber. Protection comes from surrounding conductive paths (Faraday cage), not insulation.

📞

Indoor Risk

Avoid corded phones, plumbing, and concrete floors during a storm. Lightning energy travels easily through metal pipes, electrical wiring, and the steel rebar embedded in concrete.
