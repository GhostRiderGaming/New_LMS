Biological Application

Gas Exchange in Lungs: In the alveoli, oxygen diffuses into the blood because its concentration is higher in the lungs. Simultaneously, carbon dioxide diffuses out of the blood into the lungs to be exhaled.

Common Misconception

"Particles stop moving at equilibrium."

False. At equilibrium, particles continue moving randomly. However, the net flow is zero because particles cross the boundary in both directions at equal rates.
