Interesting Fact

Some seeds can remain dormant for thousands of years. A Judean date palm seed found in Masada successfully germinated after 2,000 years of dormancy, waiting for the right moisture and temperature.

Modern Applications

Understanding this cycle is crucial for Precision Agriculture. Drones and sensors monitor specific growth stages to apply water and fertilizer only exactly when and where the plant biology needs it.

Common Misconception

Many believe plants get their mass mostly from the soil. In reality, the vast majority of a plant's physical mass comes from Carbon in the air (CO₂) captured during photosynthesis, not soil dirt.

Quick Revision

  • Input: Water + Light + CO₂ + Soil Nutrients.
  • Engine: Chloroplasts in leaves (Photosynthesis).
  • Process: Germination → Vegetative → Flowering → Fruiting.
  • Output: Oxygen (O₂) + Glucose/Starch (Yield).
