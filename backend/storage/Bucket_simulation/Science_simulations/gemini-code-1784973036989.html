Knowledge Base

Common Mistake

"Pollination is the same as Fertilization."
False. Pollination is merely the delivery of pollen to the stigma. Fertilization is the actual joining of the sperm and egg cells deep inside the ovary later on.

Interesting Fact

The Vanilla Problem.
The vanilla orchid is naturally pollinated by a specific Melipona bee found only in Mexico. To grow vanilla anywhere else in the world, humans must hand-pollinate every single flower using a tiny wooden stick.

Global Application

Agricultural Yield.
Over 75% of global food crops rely on animal pollination. Without bees, butterflies, and bats, crops like apples, almonds, and coffee would suffer catastrophic yield drops.
