Nature's Extremes

⚡ The Power of Plasma

Lightning is a giant spark of electricity in the atmosphere between clouds, the air, or the ground. In the early stages of development, air acts as an insulator between the positive and negative charges in the cloud and between the cloud and the ground. When the opposite charges build up enough, this insulating capacity of the air breaks down and there is a rapid discharge of electricity.

🌡️ Hotter than the Sun

A return stroke heats the air to ~30,000°C (54,000°F)—five times hotter than the surface of the sun. This explosive expansion of air causes thunder.

⚡ Energy Profile

A typical strike contains 1 billion joules of energy, enough to power a 60-watt bulb for 6 months, but released in just 30 microseconds.

🌋 The Restless Crust

An earthquake is the sudden release of strain energy in the Earth's crust, resulting in waves of shaking that radiate outwards from the earthquake source. When tectonic plates grind past each other, friction locks them together until the stress overcomes the rock's strength.

Logarithmic Scale

An Mw 7.0 earthquake doesn't just feel slightly bigger than a 6.0—it releases 32 times more energy and its waves have 10 times higher amplitude.

P vs S Waves

P-waves (compressional) arrive first and are felt as a sudden jolt. S-waves (shear) arrive later and cause the severe side-to-side shaking that destroys buildings.
