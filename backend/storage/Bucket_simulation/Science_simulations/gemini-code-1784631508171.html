Fascinating Fact

The Pineal gland in your brain is sensitive to light, even though it's deep inside your skull. It uses signals from your eyes to produce Melatonin, controlling your sleep-wake cycle based on the sun.

Common Mistake

Many people confuse the Endocrine and Exocrine systems. Endocrine glands release hormones directly into the blood. Exocrine glands (like sweat or salivary glands) release fluids through ducts outside the body or into the digestive tract.

Quick Revision

  • Hormone: Chemical messenger.
  • Gland: Organ that produces hormones.
  • Target Cell: Cell with matching receptors.
  • Negative Feedback: System turning itself off when levels are restored (like a thermostat).
