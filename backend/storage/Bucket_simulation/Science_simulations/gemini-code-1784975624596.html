Fascinating Facts & Applications

The Commutator Trick

If the commutator didn't reverse the current every half-turn, the coil would just flip 90 degrees and get stuck. The split-ring design is the genius mechanic that ensures continuous rotation.

It's Also a Generator

A DC motor and a DC generator are fundamentally the same machine. If you apply electricity, it spins (Motor). If you mechanically spin it by hand, it produces electricity (Generator). This principle is used in regenerative braking in EVs.

Lorentz Force Law

The mathematical foundation. The force (F) on the wire equals the magnetic field strength (B) times the current (I) times the length of the wire (L). F = B × I × L.
