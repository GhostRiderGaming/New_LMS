⚠️ The Sign Convention Trap

The #1 mistake students make is ignoring the Cartesian signs. Treat the optical center as (0,0) on a graph.

-X (Left)
+X (Right)
  • Real Object: Always placed left (-u).
  • Convex f: Always positive (+).
  • Concave f: Always negative (-).

Real-World Optics

📷 The Human Eye & Cameras

Both use Convex lenses to project a Real, Inverted image onto a sensor (or retina). Your brain flips the image right-side up automatically!

🔍 Magnifying Glass

When you place an object closer than the focal length (u < f) of a convex lens, it creates a Virtual, Erect, Magnified image.

Did you know?

Gravity can act as a lens! Massive galaxies bend light from objects behind them, a phenomenon called Gravitational Lensing predicted by Einstein.
