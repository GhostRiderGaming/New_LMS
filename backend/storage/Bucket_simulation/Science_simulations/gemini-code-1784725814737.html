Why This Matters

Agriculture

Farmers test soil to determine irrigation schedules. Sandy soils need frequent, light watering, while clay soils need infrequent, deep watering to prevent rot.

Civil Engineering

High water retention (clay) causes soil to expand and contract dramatically, which can crack building foundations and destroy road surfaces.

Flood Mitigation

Urban planners rely on porous soils (like loam/sand mixtures) in drainage zones to rapidly absorb stormwater and prevent flash flooding.
