Stage 1: Emission

White light travels in a straight line through the air.

Why does it bend?

Different Speeds for Different Colors

Light travels slower in glass than in air. However, it doesn't slow down equally for all colors.

  • Red light has a long wavelength. It slows down the least and bends the least.
  • Violet light has a short wavelength. It interacts more with the glass atoms, slows down the most, and bends the most sharply.

Because the prism faces are angled, this slight difference in bending angle at the entry point is magnified at the exit point, fanning the colors out.

🌈

Natural Prisms

Raindrops act as millions of tiny spherical prisms. Sunlight entering a raindrop refracts, reflects off the back, and refracts again upon exiting, creating a rainbow.

🔭

Spectroscopy

Scientists use prisms in spectrometers to analyze starlight. The resulting spectrum acts as a barcode, revealing exactly what elements the star is made of.

⚠️

Common Mistake

A prism does not "add" color to light. It only separates the colors that were already present in the white light. White light is a mixture.
