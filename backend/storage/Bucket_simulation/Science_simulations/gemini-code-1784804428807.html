Vital Application

Modern cities cannot exist without this process. A typical plant processes over 50 million gallons daily, preventing outbreaks of cholera and typhoid.

Did You Know?

Activated carbon in the filters has a massive surface area. Just one gram of it can have a surface area in excess of 3,000 square meters (a tennis court)!

Common Mistake

People often confuse Filtration (physical removal of solids) with Disinfection (chemical killing of microscopic biological threats). Both are needed.
