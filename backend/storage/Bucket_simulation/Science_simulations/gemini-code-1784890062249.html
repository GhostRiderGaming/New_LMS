The Viral Gray Area

Are viruses alive? Biologists still debate this. A virus possesses genetic material (DNA/RNA) and can evolve, but it cannot reproduce or perform metabolic functions without hijacking a host cell. Outside a host, a virus is as inert as a grain of sand.

X

Common Misclassification

Just because something moves or grows doesn't make it alive. Fire moves, consumes oxygen (respiration metaphor), and grows, but lacks cells, DNA, and purposeful reproduction. Crystals grow in caves, but through chemical accretion, not cellular division.
