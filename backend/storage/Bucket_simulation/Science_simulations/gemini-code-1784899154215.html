Anatomy of a Nephron

The kidney contains millions of these functional units. Each nephron filters blood, reclaims necessary nutrients, and expels waste.

How It Works

Urine formation is a continuous biological cycle across 4 distinct stages. Watch the mechanism below.

1. Ultrafiltration

High blood pressure forces water, glucose, salts, and urea out of the glomerular capillaries into Bowman's capsule. Large proteins and blood cells stay behind.

Did You Know?

Your kidneys filter about 180 liters of fluid every single day! However, 99% of that is reabsorbed, resulting in only 1.5 to 2 liters of actual urine.

⚠️

Common Mistakes

  • Thinking urine is directly filtered from the stomach or intestines. (It comes strictly from blood plasma).
  • Believing glucose in urine is normal. (It's entirely reabsorbed; presence indicates diabetes).
🧠

Quick Revision

  • Glomerulus: Filters plasma.
  • PCT: Reabsorbs nutrients.
  • Loop of Henle: Reabsorbs water & salts.
  • DCT: Secretes toxins/drugs.
  • Collecting Duct: Final water concentration.
