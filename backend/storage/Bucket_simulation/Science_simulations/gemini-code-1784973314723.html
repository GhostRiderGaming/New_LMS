Understanding Hormonal Control

The menstrual cycle is a feedback loop between the brain (pituitary gland) and the ovaries. The brain releases FSH and LH to stimulate the ovaries. In response, the ovaries release Estrogen and Progesterone to prepare the uterus.

If pregnancy does not occur, hormone levels crash. This sudden drop in Progesterone is the mechanical trigger that causes the uterine lining to shed, starting the cycle anew.

⚠️

Common Misconceptions

  • Myth: Cycles are always exactly 28 days.
    Fact: Normal cycles range from 21 to 35 days. The timeline here is an average model.
  • Myth: Ovulation always happens on day 14.
    Fact: Ovulation generally occurs 14 days before the next period begins. In a 32-day cycle, it happens around Day 18.
  • Myth: You can only get pregnant on the day of ovulation.
    Fact: Sperm can survive for up to 5 days, creating a "fertile window" leading up to ovulation.
