Beneath our feet lies a hidden ocean. The Water Table is the invisible boundary underground separating the dry, air-filled soil near the surface from the deeply soaked rock and gravel below. It is the surface of groundwater, shifting dynamically with rain and human usage.

Fossil Water

Some deep aquifers contain "fossil water" that seeped underground thousands of years ago. If pumped, they do not recharge in a human lifetime.

❌

"Underground Rivers"

A common mistake is thinking groundwater flows like rivers in vast caverns. In reality, 99% of groundwater sits in tiny pores between grains of sand and rock, like water in a sponge.

📉

Land Subsidence

When too much water is pumped out, the empty spaces in the soil collapse under the weight of the earth above, causing the ground level to permanently sink.
