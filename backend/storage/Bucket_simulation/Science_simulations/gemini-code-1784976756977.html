Absolute Zero

At 0 Kelvin (-273.15°C), theoretical particle motion stops entirely. While impossible to reach perfectly, scientists have cooled atoms to fractions of a degree above it, creating strange states like Bose-Einstein condensates.

🎈

Common Mistake

"Particles expand when heated."
False! The particles themselves (atoms/molecules) do not change size. The space between them increases because higher kinetic energy causes them to push away from each other.

🚀

Real-World App

Combustion Engines: By igniting a liquid fuel, it rapidly turns into a high-energy gas. The sudden increase in particle velocity and pressure forces pistons to move, driving the vehicle forward.
