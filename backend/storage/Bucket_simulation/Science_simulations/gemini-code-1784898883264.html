Interesting Fact

The surface area of your small intestine is about the size of a tennis court! This is due to tiny finger-like projections called villi and microvilli that maximize nutrient absorption.

Common Mistake

Many believe the stomach does most of the digestion. In reality, while the stomach churns and breaks down proteins, the small intestine is where the vast majority of chemical digestion and nutrient absorption occurs.

Quick Revision

Mechanical Digestion: Chewing (mouth) and churning (stomach).
Chemical Digestion: Enzymes breaking bonds (saliva, gastric juice, pancreatic juice).
Absorption: Nutrients entering blood (small intestine), water reabsorbed (large intestine).
