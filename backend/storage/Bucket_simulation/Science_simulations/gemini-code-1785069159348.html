Knowledge Check

Quick facts and common misconceptions to solidify your understanding.

🌿

Plant vs Animal

While we explored an animal cell, plant cells have unique organelles: Chloroplasts (for photosynthesis) and a rigid Cell Wall for structure.

🧬

Mitochondrial DNA

Mitochondria have their own distinct DNA, separate from the nucleus. This supports the theory that they were once independent bacteria swallowed by an early cell!

❌

Common Mistake

"The ER creates all proteins." Not quite. The Rough ER makes proteins destined for the membrane or export. Free-floating ribosomes make proteins used inside the cytoplasm.
