Understanding the Impact

🌍

Nutrient Cycling

Biodegradable materials break down into simple compounds like CO2, water, and biomass (humus). This returns essential nutrients to the soil, supporting new plant growth in a closed-loop natural cycle.

☠️

Microplastic Threat

Non-biodegradable plastics don't truly disappear. UV light breaks them into microscopic fragments (microplastics) which infiltrate waterways, the food chain, and eventually human bodies.

🏭

Bioplastics Myth

Not all "bioplastics" are biodegradable in nature. Many require industrial composting facilities with sustained high heat (60°C+) to break down, acting just like normal plastic if littered in the ocean.
