The Physics

Forces at Play

Why do things sink? It's a constant battle between three physical forces acting on every particle suspended in a fluid.

1
2
3
4
  • 1

    Solid Particle

    The mass inside the fluid. Its size and density determine behavior.

  • 2

    Gravity ($F_g$)

    Pulls the particle downwards. Heavier particles experience stronger pull.

  • 3

    Buoyancy ($F_b$)

    Upward push of the displaced fluid. Opposes gravity.

  • 4

    Fluid Drag ($F_d$)

    Friction from the liquid resisting movement. Increases with speed and viscosity.

Real-World Use

Water Treatment: Massive settling tanks allow dirt and floc to sink before water is filtered and sent to homes. It's the cheapest way to remove large impurities.

Geological Masterpieces

When rivers slow down upon entering an ocean, they lose energy and drop their sediment. Over thousands of years, this creates massive, fertile landforms called Deltas.

Common Misconception

"Light things never settle." False! Given enough time and assuming they are denser than the liquid, even microscopic clay particles will eventually settle. It just might take days or weeks without chemical help.
