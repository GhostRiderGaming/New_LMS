Key Insights

pH Normal vs Acid

Normal rain is slightly acidic (pH ~5.6) due to natural CO₂. Acid rain typically has a pH between 4.2 and 4.4.

⛨ Human Safety

Walking in acid rain won't burn your skin. The acids are dilute. The real harm to humans comes from inhaling the fine sulfate and nitrate particles in the air.

🏛 Cultural Decay

Acid rain reacts with Calcium Carbonate (CaCO₃) found in marble and limestone, slowly dissolving historical statues and buildings (Gypsum formation).

⚖ Recovery

Thanks to regulations like the Clean Air Act (scrubbers in smokestacks, catalytic converters in cars), acid rain in North America and Europe has decreased significantly since the 1990s.
