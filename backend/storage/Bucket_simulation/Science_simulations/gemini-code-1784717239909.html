Scientific Insights

💡

Compensation Point

At low light levels, the rate of photosynthesis equals the rate of respiration. The plant survives but doesn't grow because net glucose production is zero.

🏢

Greenhouse Optimization

Commercial greenhouses pump extra CO₂ (up to 1000 ppm) and use artificial lighting to push plants past natural environmental limits, massively increasing crop yields.

⚠️

Common Misconception

Many believe plants get their mass from soil. In reality, the physical mass of a tree comes almost entirely from Carbon Dioxide pulled out of the invisible air.
