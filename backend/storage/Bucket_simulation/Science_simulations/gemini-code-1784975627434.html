Bioaccumulation

While energy decreases up the chain, toxins (like pesticides) do the opposite. They accumulate and concentrate in apex predators, a process called biomagnification.

⚠️

Common Mistake

Arrows in a food chain diagram do NOT show "who eats whom". They show the direction of energy flow (from eaten to eater).

♻️

The Unsung Heroes

Decomposers (fungi and bacteria) aren't always shown in simple linear chains, but they connect to EVERY level, breaking down dead matter and recycling nutrients back to producers.
