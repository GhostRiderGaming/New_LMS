Kinetic to Electric

How invisible air currents power our modern world.

A wind turbine is an engineering marvel that converts the kinetic energy of moving air into mechanical energy, and finally into electrical power. Modern utility-scale turbines are massive structures, with blades sweeping an area larger than a football field, precisely engineered to extract maximum energy from the wind.

Offshore vs Onshore

Offshore turbines can be much larger and capture stronger, more consistent ocean winds, but cost significantly more to install and maintain via specialized vessels.

⚠️

Common Misconception

"They kill more birds than anything else."
While avian collisions occur, studies show house cats, buildings, and fossil fuel pollution cause millions of times more bird fatalities annually than wind turbines.

⚡

Betz's Limit

According to fluid dynamics laws formulated by Albert Betz in 1919, no wind turbine can capture more than 59.3% of the kinetic energy in wind. Modern turbines hit ~70-80% of this theoretical maximum.
