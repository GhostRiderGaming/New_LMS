Blood Flow Simulation

Connective tissues have cells suspended in a non-cellular matrix. Blood is unique because its matrix (plasma) is fluid.

Applications & Facts

Interesting Fact

The human body contains roughly 30 trillion human cells, but bone tissue is completely completely renewed every 10 years through specialized osteoclasts and osteoblasts.

Engineering Application

Understanding epithelial cell packing (scutoids) has inspired new ways to engineer flexible 3D organic electronics and wearable medical sensors that bend with the skin.

Common Mistake

Thinking "connective tissue" only means tendons. Blood and bone are both connective tissues because they consist of scattered cells suspended in an extracellular matrix.
