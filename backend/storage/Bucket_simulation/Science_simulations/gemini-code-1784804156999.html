The Engine of Life:
Nutrient Recycling

Matter is never created or destroyed in nature. It is endlessly transformed, flowing through a continuous loop connecting the living and non-living world.

The Biosphere Actors

Every element in an ecosystem plays a specific role in keeping nutrients moving. Nothing is wasted.

Producers (Plants)

Consumers (Animals)

Eat producers or other consumers to acquire organic nutrients for energy and growth.

3

Decomposers (Fungi/Bacteria)

Break down dead organic matter and waste, unlocking chemical bonds.

4

Abiotic Reservoirs

Non-living storage (Soil, Oceans, Atmosphere) holding inorganic minerals.

💧

Eutrophication

When excess fertilizer (Nitrogen/Phosphorus) washes off farms into lakes, it causes massive algae blooms. This disrupts the natural cycle, eventually starving the water of oxygen and creating "dead zones."

🦖

Ancient Atoms

Because atoms are constantly recycled, the carbon atoms currently forming your cells were likely once part of a dinosaur, an ancient fern, or dissolved in a prehistoric ocean.

❌

Common Myth

"Things just disappear when they rot."
False. Rotting is an active biological process of conversion. The mass is converted into gases (like CO2), water, and soil minerals.
