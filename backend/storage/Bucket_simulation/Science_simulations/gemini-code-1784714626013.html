Insights & Clarifications

Common Mistake: Earth's Shadow

Many believe Moon phases are caused by Earth's shadow falling on the Moon. This is false. Earth's shadow only causes Lunar Eclipses, which happen rarely. Phases are simply our view of the Moon's permanent day/night sides.

Tidal Locking (The Dark Side)

The Moon rotates on its axis at the exact same speed it orbits Earth. Because of this sync, the same side of the Moon always faces Earth. We never see the "far side" from the ground.

Earthshine

During the thin Crescent phases, you can sometimes faintly see the dark part of the Moon. This is "Earthshine"—sunlight reflecting off Earth's oceans and clouds, hitting the Moon, and bouncing back to our eyes.

The Terminator

The line dividing the light and dark sides of the Moon is called the terminator. If you stood there, you would be experiencing a very slow Lunar sunrise or sunset.
