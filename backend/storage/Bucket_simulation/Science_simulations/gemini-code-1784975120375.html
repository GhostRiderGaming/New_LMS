Ohm's Law in Action

Resistance ($\Omega$) acts as a bottleneck. As you increase resistance, collisions between electrons and the atomic lattice increase, reducing current flow and generating heat.

Knowledge Base

Applications

Resistors are used to drop voltage, limit current for LEDs, bias transistors, and act as pull-up/pull-down elements in digital logic circuits.

Did You Know?

The color sequence (Black to White) follows the visible light spectrum for the middle colors (Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Violet) mapping to numbers 2-7.

Common Mistake

Reading from the wrong end. Always start reading from the side where bands are closer together. The gap separates the multiplier from the tolerance band.
