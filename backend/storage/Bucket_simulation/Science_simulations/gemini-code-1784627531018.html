Traditional Methods: The Rahat (Persian Wheel)

Modern Methods: Drip Irrigation

Drip (or trickle) irrigation delivers water directly to the root zone of plants via a network of valves, pipes, tubing, and emitters. It is the most efficient method available today.

Common Misconception

"More water means better plant growth."

False. Flooding a field removes oxygen from the root zone, literally "drowning" the plant. Roots require a precise balance of moisture and air. Drip irrigation maintains this exact balance (field capacity), whereas traditional methods create cyclical extremes of flooding and drought.

Quick Revision

  • 🟢 Traditional (Rahat/Moat): Relies on gravity/animal power. High volume, low precision. ~40-50% efficiency.
  • 🟢 Sprinkler: Pressurized spray simulating rain. Better coverage but vulnerable to wind and high evaporation. ~70% efficiency.
  • 🟢 Drip: Pressurized micro-tubing. Water delivered directly to roots drop by drop. Minimizes weed growth. ~90-95% efficiency.
