Why not at the Equator?

Despite having the warmest water, cyclones almost never form within 5 degrees of the equator. The Coriolis force (which causes the spin) is zero at the equator and increases towards the poles. Without spin, low pressure fills in quickly without organizing.

🔥

The Latent Heat Engine

A mature hurricane releases heat energy at a rate of 50 to 200 exajoules per day. This is equivalent to exploding a 10-megaton nuclear bomb every 20 minutes. It's driven entirely by water vapor turning back into liquid (condensation).

✂️

The Enemy: Wind Shear

Vertical wind shear (change in wind speed/direction with height) is toxic to cyclones. Strong shear tilts the storm, tearing apart the organized central updraft and preventing the heat engine from stacking vertically.

📉

Common Misconception

People often think the eye is dangerous because it's the center. The eye is actually calm, clear, and sinking air. The true danger lies in the Eyewall immediately surrounding it, where wind speeds and rainfall are at absolute maximums.
