The Invisible Connection

For centuries, electricity and magnetism were thought to be separate forces. In 1820, an accidental classroom demonstration proved otherwise. When an electric current flows through a wire, it generates a magnetic field in concentric circles around it. This is the foundation of electromagnetism.

Real-World Applications

Electromagnets

Coiling the wire multiplies the magnetic field. Used in scrap yard cranes, MRI machines, and particle accelerators.

Electric Motors

The interaction between the wire's magnetic field and permanent magnets creates physical rotation to drive machines.

Loudspeakers

Varying audio current creates a varying magnetic field, rapidly moving a cone back and forth to generate sound waves.

Quick Revision

  • A straight wire carrying current produces a magnetic field in the shape of concentric circles.
  • The Right-Hand Grip Rule determines direction: Thumb points to current, fingers curl in direction of the magnetic field.
  • Field strength is directly proportional to the current ($I$).
  • Field strength is inversely proportional to distance ($r$) from the wire.
  • Reversing the current's direction exactly reverses the magnetic field's direction.
