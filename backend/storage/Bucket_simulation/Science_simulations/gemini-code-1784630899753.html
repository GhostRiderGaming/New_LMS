The Albedo Effect

Forests are dark and absorb sunlight. When cut down, lighter soil reflects more heat. However, the loss of evaporative cooling (transpiration) outweighs this, resulting in a net massive local temperature spike.

Common Misconception

"Deforestation only affects the area cut down." False. The Amazon generates its own rain; cutting portions disrupts the "Flying Rivers" (atmospheric vapor), causing droughts thousands of miles away.

Soil Liquefaction

Without root systems, heavy rainfall doesn't penetrate the ground. It runs off the surface rapidly, washing away nutrient-rich topsoil and triggering catastrophic mudslides downstream.
