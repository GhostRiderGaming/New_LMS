Two Paths to Power

Respiration is the process of breaking down glucose to create ATP, the universal energy currency of cells. Depending on the availability of oxygen, the cell takes one of two distinct chemical pathways.


Aerobic

Uses Oxygen. Slow but highly efficient. Yields massive ATP. Occurs in mitochondria.

Anaerobic

No Oxygen. Fast but inefficient. Yields very little ATP. Creates lactic acid.

Yeast & Bread

Anaerobic respiration isn't just for tired muscles. Yeast performs a type called alcoholic fermentation. Instead of lactic acid, it produces ethanol and CO₂. The trapped CO₂ bubbles are what cause bread dough to rise!

Common Mistake

Many think breathing (inhaling/exhaling) is the same as cellular respiration. Breathing is just the mechanical process of bringing oxygen to the blood. Cellular respiration is the chemical process happening inside cells.
