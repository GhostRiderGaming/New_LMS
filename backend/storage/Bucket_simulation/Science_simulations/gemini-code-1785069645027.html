Anatomy of a Motion Graph

Every line, curve, and enclosed space contains physical meaning. Hover the markers to decode.

Y-Axis (Dependent Variable)

Typically Distance (m) or Velocity (m/s). Shows what is changing over time.

2

X-Axis (Independent Variable)

Always Time (s). Progresses uniformly from left to right.

3

Slope (Gradient: Δy/Δx)

On d-t graph: Velocity. On v-t graph: Acceleration. Steeper = Faster rate of change.

4

Area Under Curve

On a Velocity-Time graph, the shaded area represents total Displacement (Distance traveled).

Common Mistake

Assuming a straight diagonal line on a velocity-time graph means constant distance. False! A diagonal v-t line means changing velocity (constant acceleration). The distance is actually curving upwards.

Quick Revision Rule

  • • d-t Slope = Velocity
  • • v-t Slope = Acceleration
  • • v-t Area = Distance (Displacement)
  • • d-t Area = Meaningless!
