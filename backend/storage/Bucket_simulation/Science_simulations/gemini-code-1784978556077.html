What is a Mole?

In daily life, we use words to group numbers. A "pair" is 2. A "dozen" is 12. In chemistry, atoms are so incredibly small that counting them by dozens is impossible. Instead, we use a much larger grouping word: The Mole.

1 Dozen

= 12 items

vs

1 Mole

= 6.022 × 10²³ items

(Avogadro's Number)

Real-world Application

Medicine Dosage: Pharmacologists use moles to ensure proper ratios of active ingredients react correctly in the body, independent of their individual atomic weights.

Mind-blowing Fact

If you had exactly one mole of unpopped popcorn kernels, it would cover the entire continental United States in a pile roughly 9 miles (14 km) deep.

Common Mistake

Confusing Moles of Atoms vs Moles of Molecules. 1 mole of Oxygen gas ($O_2$) contains 2 moles of Oxygen atoms. Always check the chemical formula!
