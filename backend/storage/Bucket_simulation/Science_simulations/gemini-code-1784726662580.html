Body Heat

Cellular respiration is about 40% efficient at capturing energy as ATP. The remaining 60% is released as heat, which is exactly how warm-blooded animals maintain body temperature.

🏃‍♂️

Lactic Acid Burn

During intense exercise, your muscles can't get Oxygen fast enough. The ETC stops. Cells switch to Anaerobic Fermentation, producing a tiny bit of ATP and Lactic Acid (causing the burning sensation).

☠️

Cyanide Poisoning

Cyanide is deadly because it binds to Complex IV in the ETC, permanently blocking Oxygen from accepting electrons. The entire turbine stops immediately, starving cells of ATP.

Common Mistake: Respiration vs. Breathing

People often use "respiration" to mean breathing (inhaling/exhaling). Breathing is just ventilation—moving air in and out. Cellular Respiration is the chemical process happening inside trillions of cells using the Oxygen you just breathed in.

Common Mistake: Plants only do Photosynthesis

Many think plants make food and animals burn it. False! Plants perform Photosynthesis to make glucose, but they also possess mitochondria and perform Cellular Respiration day and night to use that glucose to grow.
