Modern Applications & Insights

Precision GPS

Modern tractors use RTK GPS for sub-inch accuracy, preventing overlap, saving fuel, and reducing soil compaction through controlled traffic farming.

ISOBUS Standard

A universal protocol allowing the tractor's terminal to control any brand's implement, turning complex machinery into plug-and-play devices.

Common Mistake

Excessive tire pressure. Farmers often over-inflate tires, which decreases traction footprint, increases wheel slip, and damages soil structure.
