Biological Energy Flow

Food Source Explorer

Every bite you eat is transformed solar energy. Discover how nature captures sunlight in plants and how that energy flows through animals to sustain human life.

Primary Sources

Plant-Based Sources (Producers)

Plants are autotrophs; they create their own food using sunlight. We eat different anatomical parts of various plants.

1

Roots

Absorb water/minerals (e.g. Carrots, Beetroot).

2

Stem

Transports nutrients (e.g. Sugarcane, Celery).

3

Leaves

Perform photosynthesis (e.g. Spinach, Cabbage).

4

Fruit

Stores sugars and seeds (e.g. Apple, Tomato).

5

Seeds

Energy store for new plants (e.g. Wheat, Rice).

Animal-Based Sources (Consumers)

Animals consume plants (or other animals) to build proteins and fats. We obtain food either from the animal itself or what it produces.

1

Dairy (Milk)

Secreted by mammals (Cows, Goats) to feed young.

2

Meat (Beef/Pork)

Muscle tissue of mammals providing high protein.

3

Eggs

Laid by birds (Poultry) containing embryonic nutrients.

4

Poultry Meat

Muscle tissue of birds (Chicken, Turkey).

Interesting Fact

Honey is a unique food! It's an animal product (made by bees), but its source material is entirely plant-based (flower nectar).

❌

Common Mistake

Thinking "only meat has protein". Many plant sources like soybeans, lentils, and quinoa contain high amounts of protein.

🔄

Quick Revision

Plants (Producers) turn sunlight into food. Animals (Consumers) eat plants or other animals. All human food energy originates from the Sun.
