The Cosmic Identity Game

Every atom in the universe is defined by what's inside its dense center: the nucleus. But what happens when we tweak the recipe? We get families of atoms that look similar but act differently.

Isotopes

Same element, different mass.

12 C 6
14 C 6

Identical Protons (6), varying Neutrons (6 vs 8). They share chemical properties but have different physical stability.

Isobars

Different elements, same mass.

40 Ar 18
40 Ca 20

Different Protons (18 vs 20), but their total Mass Number (40) is identical. Entirely different chemical properties.

Real-World Impact

🦴

Carbon Dating

Living things absorb Carbon-12 and Carbon-14 (isotopes). After death, unstable C-14 decays. By measuring the ratio, scientists can date ancient fossils.

🏥

Medical Tracers

Isotopes like Iodine-131 emit detectable radiation. Doctors inject them to image internal organs (like the thyroid) and target cancer cells precisely.

⚡

Beta Decay

Isobars often result from Beta decay. When a neutron in Carbon-14 turns into a proton, it becomes Nitrogen-14. Same mass, new element!
