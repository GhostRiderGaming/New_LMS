The Reactivity Series

The rule is simple: A metal higher up in the series can displace a metal lower down from its solution. The reverse is never true.

MOST REACTIVE ➔ LEAST REACTIVE

Real-world Applications

  • Galvanization: Coating iron with zinc. Since zinc is more reactive, it sacrifices itself (corrodes first) to protect the iron structural integrity.
  • Extraction of Metals: Carbon (more reactive than iron) is used in blast furnaces to displace iron from iron oxide ores.

Common Mistakes

Myth: Any metal can displace any other metal.
Fact: Only a more reactive metal can displace a less reactive one. Cu + FeSO₄ = No Reaction.
Myth: The solid deposit is rust.
Fact: The brown deposit on the iron nail is pure copper metal, not rust (iron oxide).
