Lightning energy breaks N₂ bonds, creating atmospheric nitrates.

The Haber Process

In 1909, Fritz Haber invented a way to artificially fix nitrogen using extreme heat and pressure. Today, this industrial process consumes ~1% of the world's energy but produces fertilizer that sustains half the human population.

Common Misconception

Myth: Plants breathe in Nitrogen through their leaves like CO₂.

Reality: Despite being surrounded by N₂ gas, leaves cannot absorb it. Plants must absorb "fixed" nitrogen (nitrates/ammonium) through their roots from the soil.

Crop Rotation

Farmers alternate planting nutrient-demanding crops (like corn) with legumes (like soybeans or clover). The legumes replenish the soil's nitrogen naturally, reducing the need for synthetic chemical fertilizers.
