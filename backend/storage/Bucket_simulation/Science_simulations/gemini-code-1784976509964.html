Key Insights

💧

Energy Conversion

It takes 3 steps: Potential (stored water) → Kinetic (flowing water) → Mechanical (spinning turbine) → Electrical (generator).

⚖️

Efficiency

Hydropower is incredibly efficient. Modern plants can convert over 90% of available energy into electricity, compared to ~50% for fossil fuel plants.

⚡

Base Load Power

Unlike solar or wind, hydro plants can adjust output instantly to meet demand by simply opening or closing the intake gate.

❌

Common Mistake

Thinking the water enters the electrical grid. The water simply spins magnets; it remains isolated and exits safely downriver.
