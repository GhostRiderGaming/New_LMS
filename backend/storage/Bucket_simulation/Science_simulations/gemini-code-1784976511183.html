Energy Transformation

A thermal power plant does not "create" energy; it transforms it through four distinct stages:

  1. Chemical (Stored in coal bonds)
  2. Thermal (Heat in high-pressure steam)
  3. Mechanical (Kinetic energy of spinning turbine)
  4. Electrical (Electromagnetic induction in generator)

Thermodynamic Limits

Due to the Second Law of Thermodynamics, no thermal plant is 100% efficient.

Modern plants peak around 40-45% efficiency. Over half the energy generated is inevitably lost as low-grade heat, mostly expelled through the cooling tower. This is a fundamental law of physics, not just an engineering flaw.
