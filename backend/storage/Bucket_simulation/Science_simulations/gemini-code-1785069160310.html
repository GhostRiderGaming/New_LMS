★ Fascinating Facts

Endosymbiotic Theory: Chloroplasts and mitochondria have their own DNA and double membranes. Scientists believe they were once free-living bacteria swallowed by an early cell millions of years ago!

Cytoplasmic Streaming: The cytoplasm isn't static. It constantly flows in a circular motion (cyclosis) to transport nutrients and position chloroplasts for optimal light absorption.

✖ Common Mistakes

Mistake: "Plants only photosynthesize and don't respire."
Correction: Plants have mitochondria too! They photosynthesize (make food) during the day, but they respire (burn food for energy) 24/7, just like animals.

Mistake: "The cell wall controls what goes in and out."
Correction: The cell wall provides rigid structure. The thin, flexible cell membrane just inside it acts as the actual gatekeeper.
