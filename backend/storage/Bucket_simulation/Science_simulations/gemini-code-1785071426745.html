Understanding Power

Power is the rate at which energy is transferred or work is done. In electronics, it determines how bright a bulb glows or how hot a heater gets.

P = V × I

Power (Watts) = Voltage (Volts) × Current (Amps)

The Core Variables

To understand Power, you must first understand the elements that create it.

V

Voltage (Volts)

The Pressure or Push. It's the electrical force that causes free electrons to move from one atom to another. Think of it as the water pressure in a pipe.

I

Current (Amps)

The Flow Rate. It is the volume of electrons passing through a point per second. Think of it as the amount of water flowing through the pipe.

Ω

Resistance (Ohms)

The Obstruction. It is the opposition to the flow of current. The filament in a bulb has high resistance, which causes friction, generating heat and light.
