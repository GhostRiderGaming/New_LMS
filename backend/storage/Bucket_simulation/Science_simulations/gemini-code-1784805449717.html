The Anatomy of Edibles

A plant is a highly organized system. Depending on the species, different parts act as storage vaults for energy (food).

Did you know?

Botanically speaking, a Tomato is a fruit (a berry, specifically!) because it develops from the ovary of a flower and contains seeds. Culinarily, we treat it as a vegetable due to its savory flavor profile.

✕

Common Mistake

Many people think a Potato is a root because it grows underground. It is actually a tuber—a modified, swollen, underground stem designed to store starch!
